What we do

Styling

What to wear, what to keep, what to let go.

From red carpets to everyday dressing, we style individuals through a curated selection of emerging Central European designers and brands — helping you rethink your wardrobe and find a style that goes beyond trends.

Matchmaking

The right people for the right project.

We connect brands and cultural institutions with emerging talents and art creatives, sparking collaborations where art, culture and marketing meet and enrich one another.

Art direction

Where ideas become visual language.

We bring together creatives to shape your concepts, visual identities and narratives across fashion, film, art and culture — turning ideas into distinctive visual languages and stories.

Events

Bringing people, ideas and culture into the same space.

What we teach

Public

How to look at fashion, culture and creativity differently and become a more conscious participant in it. How to embrace designer fashion without intimidation, build an authentic, stylish and sustainable wardrobe beyond the trends, and learn the basics of making and mending your own clothes.

Companies

We help companies bring cultural relevance into their marketing strategies: how to work with emerging talents, understand new creative generations and communicate beyond conventional marketing. We explore the role of styling and visual self-presentation in professional contexts, and how fashion can become a meaningful part of how a company communicates — because the way a company looks, speaks and participates in culture is part of how it communicates.

Creatives

We will teach you what school often leaves out: how to make a living from your creativity without losing the reason you started. From pricing your work and building a personal brand to finding sustainable business models and creating meaningful connections with local brands, we share the practical knowledge needed to turn creative practice into a viable career.
